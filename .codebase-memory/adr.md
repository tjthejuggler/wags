# ADR addendum (2026-08-31): rewind via seekTo(0), not skipToPrevious

sendPauseAndRewindCommand now performs transportControls.pause() + transportControls.seekTo(0) instead of skipToPrevious. PREVIOUS on a freshly-staged context jumps Spotify out of the staged tracks; seekTo(0) while paused keeps the context and guarantees the song restarts from the beginning when the hold starts (both after the ~2s staging preview and at end-of-hold). Verified on device.
